agda-optimization load-bearing — definitions that support the most valuable results

USAGE:
  agda-optimization load-bearing <graph.json> [options...]

OPTIONS:
  --results=tagged|exported|terminals  result-set selector (default exported)
  --weight=unit|loc                    span weighting (default unit)
  --top-n=N                            rows to keep (default 50)
  --exclude-name-regex=PATTERN         POSIX-ERE on unqualified name (default ^[_─═]+$)

Plus the global flags --graph FILE, --format human|json, --json, --out FILE,
--explain/--no-explain and --config FILE (all usable in any position, before
or after the subcommand). The graph may also come from 'graph:' under
'global:' in the config, in which case <graph.json> can be omitted. YAML
defaults under the 'load-bearing:' section override defaults; CLI flags
override config.
See README.md for per-flag semantics.
